Unanswered quotes

Send a voice note on WhatsApp as you leave the job. A minute later the customer has a PDF quote, and Argus checks in two days later if they've gone quiet. When they accept, you get a ping. Two weeks free.

Not a chatbot. Not another app to check. It's the follow-up you'd do yourself if you had your evenings back, done systematically. It only interrupts you when a human is needed.
